```html
<section class="mt-10">
  <h2 class="text-xl font-semibold text-white">6. Browser Extension Data Practices</h2>
  <p class="text-slate-300 mt-4">
    When you use the IntelliFill browser extension, the following data practices apply in addition
    to the above:
  </p>
  <ul class="text-slate-300 mt-4 space-y-2 list-disc list-inside">
    <li>
      <strong>Form Field Metadata:</strong> The extension reads form field attributes (name, label,
      type, placeholder text) on web pages you visit while the extension is enabled. This metadata
      is used solely to match fields with your stored profile data.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Data Sent to Our Servers:</strong> Field metadata is sent to our servers at
      app.intellifill.com/api/extension/infer-fields for AI-powered field matching. Only field
      attributes are sent — not the values you type into forms.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Authentication Tokens:</strong> Your login session token is stored in your browser's
      local storage (browser.storage.local). Tokens are never synced to cloud storage or shared
      across devices.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Profile Data Caching:</strong> Your profile data (name, address, and other form-fill
      information) is cached locally in your browser for up to 5 minutes to enable fast autofill.
      The cache is automatically cleared after expiry.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>No Data Selling or Sharing:</strong> Extension data is not sold, shared with third
      parties, or used for advertising purposes.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Data Removal:</strong> You can clear all extension data at any time from the extension
      popup settings, or by uninstalling the extension.
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>
```
